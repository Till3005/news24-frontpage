# Design notes

The masthead is a full-width black band with "THE WORLD" in huge white Georgia Bold serif, the classic broadsheet evocation of an international section flagship.

Directly beneath it, a deep-red strip carries the dateline "TUESDAY, 24 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE DAY IN WORLD NEWS" in bold condensed white caps, anchoring the issue to its single newsday.

Panel 1 pairs the night rocket-launch photo of the Long March with "SHENZHOU-24: PAKISTAN'S FIRST ASTRONAUT LAUNCHES", the day's lead story.

Panel 2 shows armed tactical police in Cartagena's old town under "HOSTAGE SIEGE GRIPS CARTAGENA".

Panel 3 uses flooded Bay Area streets and the skyline shot for "RECORD KING TIDES FLOOD BAY AREA".

Panel 4 frames the Great Hall of the People at dusk for "XI PURGE: POLITBURO STAR PROBED".

Panel 5 places the courthouse microphones scene over "HUAWEI CONVICTED IN U.S. COURT", while panel 6 runs the controlled-implosion dust cloud for "IOWA TOWN EVACUATES FOR AZIDE BLAST".

Each cell ends in a solid black caption bar with a red number chip and short white uppercase headline, giving every photo a uniform broadcast-style signpost that stays legible at thumbnail size.
