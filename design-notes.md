# Design notes

The masthead is a full-width black band with "THE WORLD" in huge white Georgia Bold serif, the broadsheet evocation of an international section flagship.

A deep-red strip beneath it carries the dateline "TUESDAY, 24 NOVEMBER 2026 — 30 STORIES, 8 REGIONS" in white bold condensed caps, framing the entire issue in one line.

The hero row runs two panels side by side, each center-cropped to fill its half: the night rocket launch captioned "SHENZHOU-24: PAKISTAN'S FIRST ASTRONAUT LAUNCHES" and the courthouse microphones scene captioned "HUAWEI CONVICTED IN U.S. COURT", each closed by a black caption bar with white bold caps and a small red number chip (01/02).

Below, 28 numbered stories (03-30) fill two columns of 14 rows each: thin dividers, alternating very dark gray row backgrounds, a small colored region chip (ASIA, MIDEAST, US, EUROPE, LATAM, TECH, SCIENCE, CULTURE in distinct muted hues) and a white Georgia Bold headline of up to two clean lines with ellipsis truncation.

Small red digits at each row's left edge keep the numbering legible as a continuous index down the page.

A black footer strip closes the page with "THE WORLD — the day in one page" at left and "24 NOV 2026" at right.
